Process

How I work

  1. 01

    Intro call.

    Thirty minutes, free. We skip the jargon and find the actual problem. We define the goal, the constraints, and whether I am the right person to solve it. If I am not, I will say so and point you to someone who is.

  2. 02

    Scope on one page.

    The problem, what done looks like, the timeline, and the price, on a single page. If anything changes, the page changes first. No surprises on the invoice.

  3. 03

    The build, in the open.

    You see the work as it happens, in your repos and your accounts, with a short written update every week. I advise and I execute. Nothing is revealed at the end.

  4. 04

    Yours to keep.

    Everything I build is documented, production-ready, and runs under your credentials. When I leave, nothing leaves with me. We decide the next step, or I finish the job.
