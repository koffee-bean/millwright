What a build usually covers

  • A metadata catalog. Ownership, lineage, and search across your warehouses, so people can find data and know whether to trust it.
  • Cost controls. FinOps dashboards, workload policies, auto-suspend and auto-scale tuning, tagging, and budgets, so spend has an owner.
  • A semantic layer. Governed metric definitions in one place, so dashboards and AI tools stop disagreeing about revenue.
  • A path from model to production. Feature pipelines, a model registry, scheduled scoring, and CI gates, so models stop living in notebooks.
  • Data quality you can enforce. Rules with SLAs, monitoring and alerting, and CI checks that stop bad data before it reaches a dashboard.
